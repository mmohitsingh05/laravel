Objective:Develop a basic Inventory Management using PHP. This assignment
is intended to evaluate your understanding of PHP fundamentals, database
design, and backend logic.
Project Description:Create an Inventory Management System that allows
managing categories, products, and stock movements.

Modules to Implement:
1. Category Management
a) Create category
b) List categories

2. Product Management
a) Add product
b) List products
c) View product
d) Update product
e) Delete product

Rules:
- Product quantity must not be negative.

3. Stock Management
- Add stock entry

Rules:
- Stock OUT quantity must not exceed available stock.
- Product quantity must automatically update based on stock movement.
